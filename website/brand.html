Magic Paint brand

Magic Paint feels like opening a brand-new box of paints: bright, bouncy, a little bit sparkly, and always kind. Everything here is built from the same few pieces: chunky ink outlines, sticker shadows, toy-box colors and Drip.

Positioning

Big magic for little artists.

A free, ad-free paint studio for kids 3–8 that turns every brushstroke into a small surprise, and is safe enough to hand over without a second thought.

Audience: parents of 3–8 year olds (buyers) and the kids themselves (users). Promise: instant joy, zero risk. Proof: 18 magic brushes, kaleidoscope mode, 18 coloring pages, a buddy who talks, and no ads, accounts or data.

Logo

The full lockup pairs the app icon with the wordmark. "Magic" is set in Ink and "Paint" in Coral, and a Sun sparkle sits on the final t. Keep clear space equal to the height of the "a" on all sides. Minimum width is 120 px on screen.

Meet Drip

Drip is a paint drop with big eyes and rosy cheeks. In the app, Drip changes to whatever color the child picks, names it out loud, and cheers them on.

  • Personality: warm, encouraging, a little silly. Drip never scolds and always celebrates trying.
  • Default color: Coral. Drip may take any palette color, and always keeps the Ink outline.
  • Don't: give Drip arms or legs, a mean face, or text in speech bubbles that's longer than a few words.

Shape & style

  • Outlines: 3 px Ink stroke on every card, button and sticker.
  • Sticker shadow: a hard Ink offset straight down (4–8 px). Don't use blurry drop shadows.
  • Corners: big and round. Buttons are pills, cards use 22–32 px radius.
  • Motion: springy and quick, with small hops, bobs and twinkles. Respect reduced-motion settings.
  • Background: Cloud with a soft dot grid, like paper on a playroom wall.

Voice

We sound like

  • A fun art teacher: short, warm, excited.
  • "Let's paint!" · "Wow, so many colors!" · "Oopsie! All better."
  • Plain words for parents, with clear facts on safety and privacy.

We never

  • Scold, rush or use fear ("Don't lose your work!").
  • Use jargon, dark patterns or pushy upsells.
  • Promise things the app doesn't do.

Name

Always write Magic Paint: two words, both capitalized. Don't write MagicPaint, Magic-Paint or MAGIC PAINT in running text. In Spanish materials the product name stays Magic Paint, and the app itself speaks Spanish.
