Install guide

Magic Paint installs in about a minute and then works without the internet. Pick your computer:

Mac Windows Linux iPad & Chromebook Troubleshooting

For Mac (macOS)

  1. Download MagicPaint-Mac.dmg from the download section. One file works on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
  2. Open the file and drag the Magic Paint icon onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open Magic Paint from Applications or Launchpad.
  4. The first time, macOS may say "Apple could not verify 'Magic Paint' is free of malware." Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  5. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway next to Magic Paint. Enter your password if asked.
  6. That's it. From now on it opens normally. Tip: right-click it in the Dock → Options → Keep in Dock.

Why the message? Apple asks independent apps to be registered through a paid developer program. Magic Paint isn't registered yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once.

For Windows PCs (Windows 10 & 11)

  1. Download MagicPaint-Windows-Setup.exe from the download section.
  2. Open it from your Downloads folder.
  3. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Magic Paint installs just for your user account (no admin password), opens by itself and adds a desktop shortcut.

Press F11 to switch full screen on or off, which is great for little ones.

For Linux

  1. Download MagicPaint-Linux.AppImage.
  2. Make it runnable: right-click → Properties → Permissions → Allow executing as program, or run chmod +x MagicPaint-Linux.AppImage.
  3. Double-click to start. Some distributions need the libfuse2 package for AppImages.

iPad, Chromebook & phones

  1. Open Play online in Safari or Chrome.
  2. iPad: tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Chromebook: in Chrome, open the menu and choose Install Magic Paint (or Cast, save and share → Create shortcut).
  3. Paintings are kept in that browser between visits. Use Save to download a picture.

Updating

Download the newest version from this site and install it over the old one. Nothing gets lost, and the current painting stays where it was.

Uninstalling

Mac: drag Magic Paint from Applications to the Trash. Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Magic Paint → Uninstall. Linux: delete the AppImage file.

Troubleshooting

There's no sound or voice

Check that the speaker button in the top-right corner is on, and that your computer's volume isn't muted. Drip speaks after the first tap on Let's paint!.

Mac says the app "is damaged and can't be opened"

This can happen when a download is interrupted or a browser flags the file. Delete it, download the .dmg again and follow the Mac steps above. If it still happens, open Terminal and run xattr -cr "/Applications/Magic Paint.app", then open the app again.

Where did my painting go?

Magic Paint remembers the current painting automatically. Saved pictures go where you chose in the Save window, and it suggests your Pictures folder.

Something else?

Open an issue on our project page and we'll help.
